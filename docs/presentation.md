1. Early collision-risk estimates change as new observations arrive, but operators still need about two days to plan.
2. PRISM uses ESA-style CDM histories and stops the clock 48 hours before closest approach.
3. A grouped XGBoost model forecasts final log-risk, a calibrated warning probability, and an uncertainty band.
4. Open the escalating case. Show the cutoff, the forecast, the SHAP reasons, then reveal the outcome.
5. Compare with persistence. If the model is better, say so with the laboratory MAE. If not, say the baseline is strong.
6. Close: advisory prototype, human remains in control.
