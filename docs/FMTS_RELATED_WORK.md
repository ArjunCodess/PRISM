# Focused related-work audit

This revision applies established statistical tools to a delayed reported-risk target. It does not claim to discover censoring, point-mass targets, disagreement between prediction error and decision quality, or conditional conformal calibration. DOI metadata was retrieved from Crossref in `ml/artifacts/fmts_review/verified_references.json`; publisher verification supplements the one DOI lookup that returned 404. Access date: 2 October 2026.

| Topic and verified source | What is already solved | What remains specific to delayed conjunction reports | Revision consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Detection versus quantitation: Currie, 1968, [ACS, DOI 10.1021/ac60259a007](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ac60259a007) | Detection and quantitation limits have distinct statistical meanings. | An archive atom does not supply an instrument limit or document a probability-computation rule. | Call −30 a recorded atom with unresolved generation semantics. |
| Censored regression: Tobin, 1958, [DOI 10.2307/1907382](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1907382) | A likelihood accounts for a known bound on a latent continuous outcome. | The latent-risk likelihood cannot be justified without establishing ESA's bound mechanism. | Gaussian Tobit is a standard comparison for known-clipping simulation only. |
| Two-part and hurdle models: Cragg, 1971, [DOI 10.2307/1909582](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1909582) | Separate occurrence of a boundary mass from the continuous positive component. | Here the mass is at a recorded log-risk value, and the target is a later update, not event occurrence. | Retain the existing hurdle as prior art; compare calibrated mixture mean and decision-selected hurdle. |

MAE decomposition is diagnostic. |
| Decision-focused forecasting: Elmachtoub and Grigas, [Smart Predict, then Optimize, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922) | Prediction should reflect the downstream objective, with methods for optimization-aware learning. | ESA's scalar challenge score does not encode a manoeuvre optimization problem or operational costs. | Use validation selection on the challenge loss. ## Contribution boundary

The revised contribution is a reproducible aerospace benchmark extension: an explicit archive-semantics audit, an operationally selected remedy comparison, and outcome-category prediction-set evaluation with a separate decision tail. Known methods are credited. A controlled mechanism experiment separates assumptions about latent censoring from assumptions about the recorded target. General evaluation advice is background, not a novel framework.

No temporal foundation model is evaluated. The event-level feature table is not a regular forecasting series; inventing a resampling or imputation task to accommodate a pretrained forecaster would change the task. Foundation-model and model-scaling conclusions are removed.

## Second-data audit

[UCI Air Quality](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/360/air+quality) documents −200 as missing data. Treating it as a detection limit would repeat the unsupported-semantics mistake. [EPA WQX guidance](https://www.epa.gov/waterdata/wqx-detection-limits-best-practices-guide) documents real censored measurements, but does not by itself establish a licensed, fixed-limit, delayed forecasting dataset with repeated-site split integrity. Such data remain a possible future study, not an empirical result of this revision. We use simulation rather than claiming a second empirical domain. The simulation's structural-atom and missing-update variants deliberately share the same observed joint distribution; different latent meanings can produce identical recorded observations, so observed-target performance cannot resolve that semantic ambiguity.
